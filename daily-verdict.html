When does it arrive?
Every connected account is read every day, on data through the last full day. You can also ask about any account in chat, any time.
Where does the monthly amount come from?
The model's estimate from the account's own numbers, shown with the evidence. It ranks the work; it doesn't promise a saving. A finding gets no figure when the account evidence cannot support one.
How is this different from a scheduled report?
A report tells you CPA is up 18% week on week. A verdict tells you what to change and what it is costing a month, with the evidence underneath, so you can disagree with a position instead of staring at a chart. Why Adgent
I run a book of client accounts. Which one first?
Each account gets its own read. The workspace lets you switch between them and see which one needs attention first; the analysis never blends clients. For agencies
Where does a decision go next?
A completed account move enters Being measured with its date and before-state. Once the result window closes, the outcome returns to the same chain and becomes context for the next read. Change Ledger
