Planning, answered plainly.

Is media planning live?
Planning is in pilot. The plan on this page shows the shape of a plan, not a product capture or a forecast. The account-move cards, the 20% band check and the Change Ledger it hands off to are live today.
What goes into a plan?
The budget, dates and goal you set. Adgent drafts the weekly split by line with the basis for each number, one test and its guardrails. Where the account's history is thin, it leaves expected CPA blank instead of guessing.
Does the plan change the account by itself?
No. Going live is a change, so each budget step arrives as a card with its current and proposed value. Nothing moves until you approve it, and a completed move keeps the value it replaced.
Can I share the plan?
Yes. The plan is a document you can save, reopen and share from the same account context, and open again at the client review.
