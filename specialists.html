Questions about the ten.

Do I have to set them up?
No. They are built in and start from the first run of a connected account. Their decisions land in Decisions.
Where does a specialist finding go next?
It lands in Decisions with the evidence, current state and proposed move attached. Completed account moves continue into the Change Ledger with the value they replaced and their execution status.
Are the monthly amounts a promise?
No. They are model estimates, not measured savings. The first nine cards come from one live account; the Budget-Pacing Cop card is a separate illustration.
Can I add agents of my own?
Yes. Describe a check in a sentence and tie it to a condition, an event or a schedule. See Agents and workflows.
