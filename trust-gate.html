Know which numbers can carry a decision.

The Trust Gate is Adgent's rule that weak evidence cannot carry a decision. Adgent checks a number before it can rank a decision or move money. Reliable evidence advances; weak signals become a named finding.

What happens when a number can't be trusted?
It becomes a finding, not an action. With checkout-inflated ROAS, every ROAS- or CPA-ranked action in that run is held back.
Does the gate fix my tracking?
No. It names the input that failed. The fix happens in your tag setup; later reads show when the signal becomes usable.
What if pasted text contains instructions?
It is treated as data, not control. Security and data
What gets logged when a change does go through?
The value it replaced, the new value, the time and who approved it, in the Change Ledger. Edits made directly in Meta or Google are recorded in the platform change log, with a date and the actor the platform reports.
