Straight answers.

Do I upload the creative?
No. It reads the ads already running on the Meta and Google accounts you connect.
Does it make ads?
No. It reads them and briefs the next one. Making the ad stays with your team or agency.
What does the dossier feed next?
The concept view, creative brief and report all use the same hook, angle, offer, persona and performance read, so the next piece of work starts from what the ad already proved.
Can it read competitors' ads?
Not yet. Competitor creative from the ad library is on the roadmap. Today it reads your own connected accounts.
Does it flag creative fatigue before CPA moves?
It reads the early signs. Fatigue is scored on every analysed ad, and each concept is tagged Scaling, Testing or Gen exhaustion. On one live account, 30 Sep 2026, it flagged a carousel whose click-through fell 19.8% while its messaging conversions rose 16.8%: the offer still worked, the hook had worn out. The data is daily, so it reads the trend, not the hour.
