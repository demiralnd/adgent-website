The questions people ask before getting started.

What does Adgent actually do on day one?
Connect Meta and Google Ads and the first run sorts what Adgent finds into decisions, each with its evidence and an estimate of what it is worth a month, the costliest on top. On one live account the first run returned 53 decisions plus 6 on watch.
What data does it read besides my ad accounts?
GA4, Search Console, Merchant Center and the Drive files you pick add business context beside Meta and Google Ads. Merchant Center answers in chat; it is not part of the daily analysis yet. Shopify is planned.
What happens after the first account read?
Keep the decisions moving in chat, turn a recurring check into an agent, ask for a report or plan, or prepare the account change from the same working record.
