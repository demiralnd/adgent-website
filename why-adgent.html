Your agency and brand can work on the same answer.

When one person runs the AI conversation, everyone else's questions pass through them. Adgent lets the agency and brand question the same analysis together, then carries the agreed work into the next report, plan or account move.

Fair questions, short answers.

Isn't this a dashboard with a chat box?
A dashboard shows a view. Adgent brings a position with a price on it: 53 decisions on one account's first run, each with the evidence and next move attached. Decisions
Why use Adgent alongside the platform's own automation?
The platform is good at the auction, inside its own pixel and its own account. Adgent adds your margin, the other platform and the account's decision history.
How is this different from an AI assistant on my ad accounts?
Adgent keeps the account's daily history, reads every account every day (including days you don't ask), reads the creative itself and carries the decision into reports, plans and account work.
