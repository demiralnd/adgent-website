How pricing works

Before you ask for a seat at the table.

Can my team use it today?
Yes. Request a demo and we'll show how Adgent joins a team conversation, brings the account into the discussion and carries the agreed work forward.
What does Aida bring before the meeting?
The account's current numbers, the brand's confirmed goals and constraints, and the work left open from earlier decisions.
How is it different from a notetaker?
A notetaker records what happened. Aida contributes while the decision forms, then turns that decision into the report, recurring agent or account move the team asked for.
What comes out of the meeting?
A confirmed memory, assigned follow-up, report, recurring agent or account change, depending on what the team decided.
Where does the work show up after the call?
In the app and by email, with the decision, owner, evidence and status attached.
How do I get started?
See Pricing to discuss your team's workflow. Adgent carries confirmed context between chat and meetings, so the next conversation builds on the last decision.
