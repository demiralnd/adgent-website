Pricing
What is Adgent?
Adgent is an AI marketing employee for agencies and brands that run Meta and Google Ads. It learns your business from the context you confirm, joins your team's meetings with account intelligence, then carries the decisions into execution plans and account changes. A change is applied only after you approve it.
Who is Adgent for?
Agencies first: network and holding-group agencies running Meta and Google Ads for many brands, where senior hours are the limit. Then the large brands themselves and the teams that run their spend. An agency switches between client brands and sees which one needs attention first.
What does multiplayer mean here?
The agency and the client work through the same account analysis with Adgent. Both can add context, question a finding and direct the next step in the meeting. Confirmed decisions stay with the brand's work. Separate client logins and roles are not built yet. See the meeting workflow
Is everything on this site live?
Adgent's AI employee runs across account analysis, reporting, account changes and team meetings. Features still on the plan are marked Coming. Every capability, and where each one stands
What can I actually do from chat?
Ask about any campaign, ad set or ad and get the answer with its numbers. Ask for a dashboard, a media plan or a campaign draft. Describe an agent in a sentence and it is set up. Ask for a change and Adgent prepares the exact account move; ask to put one back and it prepares the inverse from the ledger.
Which platforms does it cover?
Meta and Google Ads, through their own APIs. GA4, Search Console, Merchant Center and the Drive files you choose can add context. You can share store revenue, refunds or margin in Drive; Adgent can compare the account read with that file and name the gap. Shopify is planned, not connected today.
Why not connect ChatGPT or Claude to our ad accounts ourselves?
For a one-off question, that can work. Adgent keeps day-level account data outside the chat, syncs it nightly, reads the creative and gives recurring specialists the same confirmed account context. The 28-day read, the 12-month audit and the next report stay on the same working record instead of starting over with each prompt.
How do I know an agent won't spam me?
A condition has to hold for several checks before an agent fires, there is a cooldown after it does, and the number has to recover before the same alert can come back. A metric sitting on the line does not reach you twice an hour. Runs collect in your Outputs, where you mark each one seen or dismiss it.
What does AI Visibility measure?
Whether ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews name, list or recommend you, which sources they cite and who they recommend instead. AI Visibility
We already have a dashboard. Why add another tool?
Adgent keeps the account's goals, constraints and past decisions with the data it reads. It ranks what needs attention, prepares the report or account change and carries the measured result into what happens next.
