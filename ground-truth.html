Does Adgent reconcile GA4 against my platform numbers?
No. It reads both side by side in chat; it doesn't produce a reconciled figure. Untrustworthy signals go through the Trust Gate.
Do I have to connect my store?
Not to start. Put your store's revenue, refunds or margin in a Drive file and the read sets the platform's revenue against yours. A direct Shopify connection is next.
What revenue can't it see?
Anything that reaches neither a connected source nor a file you share, like a phone order nobody logged. Where there is no basis for a figure, it gives none and says why.
Does it write to any of these sources?
No. GA4, Search Console, Merchant Center and selected Drive files add context to the account read. Account moves are limited to Meta and Google Ads and recorded in the Change Ledger. Security and data
