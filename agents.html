Questions about agents.

What happens after I describe an agent?
Adgent turns the sentence into a schedule or condition, selects the sources and sends each run to Outputs. Open any run to see the evidence and next move.
How do I build one?
Describe it in a sentence in chat, or fill in the settings yourself: name, sources, what wakes it, the instruction and where the output goes.
Won't it become another stream of alerts?
It is built to speak when its condition changes, then cool down. Runs collect in Outputs, where you mark each one seen or dismiss it.
What can an agent read?
Your Meta and Google Ads accounts, plus GA4, Search Console, Merchant Center and the Drive files you pick. What each source adds.
