Adgent is the brand and service name. The business that operates it, contracts and invoices customers, holds the advertising-platform accounts, and acts as data controller is a sole proprietorship registered in Türkiye. This page states that identity in one place.

01The registered business

Entity typeSole proprietorship (şahıs işletmesi)

A sole proprietorship in Türkiye is registered under the name of the person who owns it, so the legal business name and the owner's name are the same string. There is no separate company name, trade title or registration number behind the Adgent brand today.

The registered address is not published on this page. A sole proprietorship registers at its owner's address, so it is a private address rather than an office; it is supplied in full inside platform verification flows and to counterparties on request at the email or phone number above.

02Brand, service and website

Brand / service nameAdgent
Websitehttps://adgent.app
Operated byOsman Tıkna, sole proprietorship, Türkiye

Adgent connects to advertising accounts the customer already owns on Meta and Google Ads, reads them, and proposes changes. It is read-only by default: nothing is written to an advertising account until a person approves that specific change, and every applied change is recorded with the value it replaced. The advertising-platform applications, API access and business accounts used to deliver the service are held by the business named above.

03Data controller and applicable law

The data controller for the service is Osman Tıkna, the sole proprietorship named above, established in Türkiye. Turkish data-protection law (KVKK) applies, and the GDPR applies where its territorial scope is triggered.

What we collect, why, how long we keep it and who processes it is described in the Privacy Policy and summarised in Data Use. Deletion routes are in Data Deletion.
