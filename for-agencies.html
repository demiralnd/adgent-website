Questions agencies ask first.

The longer read: what an AI media buyer does for an agency.

How does the team know which client needs attention first?
The workspace shows every connected client as its own card, with health, spend and open items. Your team starts with the brand where the next decision is worth the most.
How does each client keep its own context?
Each brand has its own memory, account history, goals and constraints. Open a client and the next read starts with that brand's working record.
What leaves a client review?
The agreed report, board, follow-up or account move, with its evidence, owner and status attached.
What can I share with the client?
A board link or PDF. The link opens a dated snapshot that you can update or revoke. Dashboards and reports
Why not connect ChatGPT or Claude to our clients' accounts ourselves?
For a one-off question, that can work. Adgent stores each client's day-level account history outside the chat, syncs it nightly, reads the creative and gives its specialists the same confirmed brand context. The 28-day read, the 12-month audit and the next report stay on one working record.
