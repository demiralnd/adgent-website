Questions about reports.

Does the client's link update itself?
No, on purpose. The link is a dated snapshot, frozen when you share it, so the numbers your client saw are the numbers you can stand behind. Inside Adgent the board stays live; share again for fresher numbers.
Does my client need a login?
No. Anyone holding the link can open it, so send it to the people it is for and revoke it when the work is done.
What can I export?
A PDF of any shared board or report, each page stamped Live or Static. Wide tables can break across PDF pages, so the link is the clean copy. Standing agents can file output as PDF, CSV, Excel or HTML. Agents and workflows
What happens after a report finds a move?
The finding becomes a decision card with its evidence, current state and proposed state. Once completed, it enters the Change Ledger, so the next report can show what changed. Change Ledger
