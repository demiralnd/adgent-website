Built from chat

Ask for it in a sentence. Adgent builds it.

A report, a live dashboard, a cross-channel plan — described in one message and built while you watch. Not a template you fill in, and not a chart library you assemble. You say what you want to see; the thing that answers it gets made.

Why this exists

The report you actually want is never the one on the menu.

Every reporting tool ships with a set of views someone else decided you would need. The question in your head at 9am is more specific than any of them — and building it yourself costs an hour you were going to spend on the account.

Custom reports, from one message

Describe the cut you want — the platforms, the window, the breakdown, the metric you judge on — and get the report rather than a form to configure one.

Live dashboards, not exports

What gets built stays connected to the data and keeps updating. You can share it with a client or a colleague without turning it into a screenshot that is wrong by Thursday.

Campaigns drafted by description

Cross-channel structures built from what you said you wanted — and then held, complete, until you approve them. Describing is not applying.

The hour is the thing to price. One rebuilt-by-hand view a week, at the hour it costs your most expensive person, is most of a working week over a quarter — and that is only the reports that get built. The more expensive ones are the questions nobody asks because the answer would take an afternoon, which is how an account ends up run on the four views that shipped with the tool.

Asking for a view that does not exist yet
Build me a weekly report by placement, against store revenue, for this client.
Building it now. Placement breakdown across Meta and Google, weekly buckets, reconciled against recorded revenue rather than platform-reported.

One thing worth knowing before you send it: Audience Network is 22% of spend and 4% of revenue on this account. It will be the loudest thing on the chart.
Can the client see it live?
Yes — it is a live view, not an export, so it stays current after you share it. Read-only for them.

The part that makes it safe

Building something is not the same as doing it.

A drafted campaign is an artifact, not an action. It sits there, fully specified, until you approve it — and when you do, it goes through the same gates as every other change: shown with its before-state, labelled reversible or not, and logged.

And it will tell you when what you asked for will not work

Ask for a campaign built to a target the account has never reached and you get pushback with the account's own history attached, before anything is launched. Building on request does not mean building whatever is requested.
