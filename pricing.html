Questions

Plainly.

Do all plans include both platforms?
Yes. Meta and Google Ads come together — there is no "channel add-on", and no version of this that sees only one of them. What changes with scale is how many accounts are connected and whether execution is switched on, not which platforms you can read.
How is it priced?
Pricing depends on how many ad accounts you connect and whether you want execution as well as intelligence. Tell us what you're running and we'll walk you through the options.
What counts as an ad account?
One connected Meta or Google ad account equals one. A brand running multiple channels uses one slot per account; agencies connect as many as they manage.
What data does it read beyond ads?
Alongside Meta and Google Ads, Adgent reads GA4, Search Console and Merchant Center, so platform-reported numbers get checked against a source the platform does not control. Store reading (Shopify) is the next connection we are building.
Does Adgent change my campaigns on its own?
No. Access is read-only by default. When a fix or a campaign is ready, Adgent prepares it and waits — you apply it from chat. The control is always yours; nothing is automatic.
