# Ask-an-AI icons: sources and terms (internal, 2026-11-01)

All files were downloaded on 2026-11-01. Each one keeps the provider's path geometry unchanged; nothing was redrawn or traced. Sanitizing removed root `width`/`height`, `class`, comments, DOCTYPE and metadata, and added `aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"`. No file had a script, event handler, `<image>`, foreignObject or external href. None carries ids, so all can be inlined on one page. ## Usage terms the integrator must respect

- **OpenAI** (https://openai.com/brand/): "Use the logo exactly as provided and acknowledge that it belongs to OpenAI." Also: "Misrepresent your relationship with OpenAI, imply endorsement, or confuse users about sponsorship" (listed under Don't), "DON'T add any colors to the Blossom", "Use the prescribed spacing to let the Blossom breathe", and "Do not feature our Marks more prominently than your own company's name or marks." Keep it black, and leave CSS margin around the icon because the viewBox is tight.
- **Anthropic** (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/trademark-guidelines, effective Aug 1 2024): "You may only use our trademarks as specifically permitted by us and only in materials we approve beforehand." Also: "No alterations of our trademarks (changes to color, font, proportion, or otherwise) are permitted." and "Maintain a reasonable space between each side of our trademark and other graphic or textual elements." This is the strictest text of the four. It asks for prior approval and permits no colour change, so a monochrome variant is not allowed.
- **Perplexity** (https://live.standards.site/perplexity/logo): "always make sure it is clear and legible against the background, so use light colors on dark backgrounds and vise versa." Also "One Color Versions" are allowed (Offblack `#091717` would also be on-guide), "Don't fill it.", "Don't rotate it.", and "leave about a symbol's worth of space between our logo and any other elements on the page." No separate trademark-permission page was found.
- **Google / AI Mode** (https://about.google/brand-resource-center/brand-elements/#product-icons): "To use a Google product icon in your work, create a Partner Marketing Hub account to find assets and request permission through our approval form." From https://about.google/brand-resource-center/rules/: "Don't remove, distort, or alter any element of a Google Brand Feature." and "Don't display Google Brand Features in any manner that implies a relationship with, affiliation with, sponsorship by, or endorsement by Google." Plain-text use of the name is allowed: "You can refer to Google or our products in an informational context in plain text" (https://about.google/brand-resource-center/guidance/). No AI Mode-specific usage page exists. The icon is Google's own glyph next to the "AI Mode" label, so keep the label "Google AI Mode" and do not recolour beyond the documented light-theme fill.

Common to all: this is referential use only. Do not imply partnership or endorsement, do not recolour, do not distort, do not make any of these icons more prominent than Adgent's own mark, and leave clear space. Both Anthropic and Google formally ask for prior approval before their icons are used.

**Owner decision, 2026-11-01:** the icons are used without requesting approval ("ikonları kullan ya izne gerek yok"). The terms above stay recorded; if a provider objects, set that provider's `icon` to `null` in `js/ask-ai.js` and the button falls back to its name.
