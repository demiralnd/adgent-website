Can I undo an edit made in Ads Manager?
No. Those are recorded in the platform change log, not undone. Adgent can prepare the old value as a new account move.
Is putting a change back one click?
No. For supported reversible changes, the agent prepares the inverse from the recorded before-state. You approve it, and the put-back lands as its own row. Some action types cannot be reversed through Adgent.
Can I put back something from last week?
For supported reversible changes, earlier rows retain their before-state so the agent can prepare the way back for approval. A recorded history does not make every action type reversible.
Who can see the change history?
Everyone with access to that account's workspace sees the same rows, each with a date and a source. Per-person roles and seats aren't built yet. The history stays with that one company and never appears in another client's account.
How much of this has run on live accounts?
7 of 27 write action types end to end, as of 9 Sep 2026. Put-backs ran on one Meta and one Google Ads account.
