Running these accounts for clients? How Adgent works across a book of client accounts

In lead gen, the failure signal is a spike.

Every alert is built to watch for things falling. Junk leads do the opposite, and the season is too short to notice late.

The spike that looks like a win.

Spam and bot leads push the conversion count up and the cost per lead down, so the account trains itself on bots while every indicator reports success. Blocking the source afterwards stops new junk; it doesn't erase what the bidding already learned from it.

The form fill arrives late in the journey.

6sense's 2025 survey of nearly 4,000 B2B buyers puts first contact with a vendor at 61% of the way through the buying journey. The platform treats that one form fill as the conversion and optimises toward producing more of them.

Source: 6sense, 2025 Buyer Experience Report.

Hours, weeks, months.

Google can spend up to twice a campaign's average daily budget on a given day, capped at 30.4 times it over the month. After a change, a bid strategy can take up to three weeks to calibrate. Medicare's open enrolment runs 54 days, 15 October to 7 December. Whether the leads were any good arrives from the CRM weeks or months later, often after the season it belonged to.

Sources: Google Ads Help, spending limits and learning period; Medicare.gov, Open Enrollment.

The questions lead-gen teams ask first.

How do I know whether my cheap leads are any good?
Usually you can't tell from the ad account alone. Your lowest cost per lead is often your highest cost per customer, and a dashboard sorted by cost per lead will never show it. Measurement Integrity checks what the tracking is counting before any lead is counted, and a CRM export in Google Drive can be read in chat next to your campaigns.
What if my campaigns are optimising toward the wrong conversion?
That gets checked first. Primary and secondary conversion events are classified separately, so the event actually driving the bidding is visible rather than assumed. A metric window the Trust Gate can't trust may raise a finding, never an action. Trust Gate
Can it judge a channel on closed deals instead of form fills?
Not from the CRM directly; Adgent doesn't connect to one. A CRM export dropped into Google Drive is read in chat beside the campaigns, but it doesn't feed the daily analysis or send offline conversions back to the platforms. Worth knowing why nobody closes this cleanly: Google keeps a click identifier for 90 days, and B2B payback runs around 16 months. Ground truth
What happens if my tracking breaks and nobody notices?
Broken tracking is treated as a finding, not a blank space. Conversion tracking health is monitored, on Meta including the Conversions API feed, and each day's data is checked against the account's own history, so a collection that stops or a count that jumps overnight shows up the next day instead of at month-end.
