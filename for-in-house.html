Questions an in-house lead asks first.

We already have an agency. Why a second read?
Adgent gives your internal team an independent read of the same accounts, with the evidence and next move attached. Keep the agency; enter the review with your own position.
Can it see our real revenue?
It reads GA4 beside the platforms and remembers the margin figures you confirm. You can also bring store revenue, refunds and offline conversions through a Drive file. Shopify is planned. Ground truth
What does it need from my stack?
Meta and Google Ads, connected by signing in rather than an implementation project. GA4, Search Console, Merchant Center and the Google Drive files you pick add business context. Your store and CRM are not connected; Shopify is the next store connection planned.
What does my team get before the review?
A ranked account read, the evidence behind each finding and the next move already drafted. The same working record is ready when the discussion begins.
How does it separate a signal from a bad data window?
Every metric window is labelled trusted, degraded or untrusted before it is scored. Recent days inside the attribution lag or a tracking change mid-window can raise a finding but not an account action. Trust Gate
Which parts of AI Visibility are live?
Live: seeing where AI answers lose you, and weighing that against your ads and goals. Pilot: turning it into a paid plan with a message, creative or landing-page brief. Exploring and not scheduled: ads inside AI answers, ChatGPT first, and measuring them. AI Visibility
Can it join our weekly review?
Yes. It joins as Aida, with the account's data and the brand's memory, then carries the agreed work into the app and email. In your meetings
