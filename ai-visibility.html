Named is not recommended.

Every brand in every answer gets one of four levels. Only the top two count.

  1. Absent

    Not in the answer.

  2. Named only

    Mentioned, never offered as a choice.

  3. ListedCounts as recommended

    One of the options, or its own card or listing.

  4. Explicitly recommendedCounts as recommended

    Singled out, or the only option.

Counts as recommended

List order is never weighted · a brand the answer warns against never counts as recommended · no 0 to 100 score

From an AI answer to a paid decision

What the answers show is weighed against the brand's goals and its ads before it becomes a plan. A gap is not by itself a reason to spend, and an ad doesn't change what the assistant answers on its own.

  1. LiveSee where the answers lose you
  2. LiveWeigh it against your ads and goals
  3. PilotTurn it into a paid plan: message and creative or landing-page brief
  4. ExploringAds inside AI answers, ChatGPT first. Not scheduled.
  5. ExploringMeasure those ads and keep the result in memory

Also coming to AI Visibility itself: a sources page and report, accuracy checks and notifications, suggestions on the page.

Straight answers.

Will Adgent get ChatGPT to recommend us?
No vendor can promise that. Adgent measures where you are named and recommended, who wins instead, and on which sources. What to do stays your call.
Which assistants and markets?
ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews, the default logged-out answer, in one market today: Turkey, with questions asked in Turkish. Perplexity, Claude, Copilot, Meta AI and Grok are not covered, nor are brands that are themselves a marketplace or comparison site.
Why is there no visibility score?
A score hides how many answers it rests on. Each reading is a count with its dates and a 95% range, marked early below 30 answers.
Do we need ad accounts connected?
No. Without ads, setup starts from your brand name and a live search; gaps are then ranked by answers lost instead of spend.
