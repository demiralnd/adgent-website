The change log, plainly.

Can Adgent undo an edit made in Ads Manager?
No. It is recorded, not undone. Adgent can prepare the old value as a new account move. Adgent's own changes keep their before-state in the Change Ledger.
Is this real time?
Changes are fetched about every ten minutes. Spend and results are daily, so what a change did shows from the next day's read.
How far back does it go?
From today back into the account's history. Depth depends on the account and the platform, so we don't print one number.
Where do I see it?
In chat and in reports: ask what changed or why a number moved.
